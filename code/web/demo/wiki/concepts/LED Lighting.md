Photosynthetic photon flux density (PPFD) drives growth rate, and LEDs let
you tune the spectrum per crop stage. The dominant power draw in a
[[Hydroponics]] rack.

Waste heat couples straight into [[Root Zone Temperature]], so lighting and
thermal control are designed together. [[Jane Researcher]] keeps the current
spectrum recipes.
