A thin film of nutrient solution flows continuously past bare roots in a
sloped channel. A workhorse [[Hydroponics]] method for leafy greens, and the
one [[Veggie]] adapts for microgravity.

## Trade-offs

Low water volume means low thermal mass, so a pump failure shows up fast —
see [[Root Zone Temperature]]. Recovery of the returning film is covered in
[[Water Recovery]].

Measured throughput figures come from the [[Plant Growth Paper]].
